ip intelligence console
Enter an address to resolve its location, network, and connection profile. Everything runs in your browser against free public sources — no keys, nothing stored.
Full profile: geolocation, ISP/org, ASN, reverse DNS, timezone.
Capped at 50 IPs per run and throttled to stay within free rate limits.
Heuristic classification — residential vs. datacenter / hosting / likely VPN — from network ownership.
Up to 25 points. Locations are ISP-level approximations.
How to read this: geolocation is approximate — it reflects where a network is registered or hosted, usually the ISP or a datacenter, not a street address.
Identity: a public lookup can't reveal a person. Names and subscriber addresses sit only with the ISP and are released only under legal process.
Masking: VPNs, proxies and cloud hosting hide the true origin; the "Security" verdict is a best-effort guess, not proof.
Sources: ipwho.is (geo/network) · dns.google DoH (reverse DNS) · Natural Earth via world-atlas (map).
